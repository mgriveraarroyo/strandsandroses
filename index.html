About Angel

The Angel
Behind the Chair

"Gender neutral hair and lashes salon. Where you can feel welcomed, appreciated and understood."

Angel has spent years transforming how Atlanta experiences beauty — not as a transaction, but as a practice of genuine care. As a hairstylist, she works in color, extensions, and gender-neutral cuts that honor the full spectrum of identity. As a lash artist, she creates spa-like appointments where every detail is considered and every client leaves feeling like the best version of themselves.

Strands & Roses is a hair and lash studio — a space where craft and warmth are one and the same. Angel's work is guided by a single conviction: that beauty belongs to every body, every identity, every size, without exception.

Located inside My Salon Suite at 1000 Northside Drive NW, Atlanta — a private, intimate setting designed for the experience you deserve.
